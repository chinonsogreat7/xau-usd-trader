## 3. Normalized strategy hypothesis

The hypothesis is that H1 supply/demand zones and H1 market structure can identify directional
context, while an M15 zone retest followed by a reversal candle and EMA8 crossover can identify a
next-M15-open entry. Stops use the zone or an M15 swing with an ATR buffer; targets use H1
structure; trades without at least the selected reward-to-risk threshold are rejected.

The document's complete flow is a hypothesis to falsify. It is not evidence of positive
expectancy.

## 5. Explicit document hypotheses

The DOCX itself identifies these as implementation assumptions rather than presenter-stated rules:

1. ATR-based impulse thresholds.
2. Zone boundaries from the final opposite candle before the impulse.
3. Three bars on each side for pivot detection.
4. A `0.15 × ATR` stop buffer.
5. First-touch or limited-retest handling.
6. A `2.0R` baseline threshold.

They remain mutable research hypotheses. A later accepted value must be versioned and predeclared
before evaluating the applicable holdout.

## 6. Blocking semantic decisions

The following items block a faithful typed strategy and must not be filled with silent defaults:

1. Define “total upward/downward movement” for the four-candle impulse window.
2. Define ATR algorithm, seed, warm-up, current-bar inclusion, timeframe, and snapshot time.
3. Select the first baseline: the experiment matrix's simple Base, or the document's complete
   trend-plus-impulse flow.
4. Decide whether an opposite origin candle must precede the impulse window or may appear inside it.
5. Define one retest as an overlap transition/episode rather than accidentally counting every
   overlapping M15 bar.
6. Select first-touch-only versus allowing one previous retest.
7. Define selection and tie-breaking when several valid zones overlap price.
8. Decide whether confirmation may occur on the touch bar and set a confirmation-state expiry.
9. Specify EMA8 initialization and the median convention for 20 candle bodies.
10. Enumerate bearish engulfing/displacement and doji semantics rather than relying on “reverse.”
11. Define the M15 swing used by the stop, including its right-side confirmation delay.
12. Define structural-target selection, snapshot time, tie handling, and no-target behavior.
13. Define equal-timestamp ordering when an H1 close and M15 close occur together.
14. Define stop/target same-bar priority, bid/ask triggers, gaps, slippage, tick rounding, and order
    cancellation before next-open entry.
15. Define “loss,” daily P&L, weekly drawdown, timezone, reference equity, and reset boundaries.
16. Supply contract size, tick value, lot step, minimum/maximum quantity, margin policy, and gap-risk
    handling.
17. Select data/feed identifiers, exact bar boundaries, missing-bar behavior, session calendar,

18. Freeze a precise out-of-sample end date; the stated 2025–2026 interval is incomplete as of this

### Implemented diagnostic policy surface

The following previously ambiguous choices are now representable as required, versioned policy
values. This is an implementation capability, not approval of any option as the strategy baseline:

- impulse movement: net first-open-to-final-close, full four-bar range, or the sum of directional
  candle bodies;
- ATR: Wilder smoothing with an SMA seed or a rolling simple mean, with the snapshot taken before
  the impulse window, before its final bar, or at its final bar; period, directional-bar minimum,
  and both ATR multiples are also required;
- origin selection: the last opposite H1 candle strictly before the impulse window, or the last
  opposite candle at or before its end, always under a finite required lookback;
- retest freshness: first episode only or one prior episode allowed, where consecutive inclusive
  M15 overlaps are one episode;
- equal-time precedence: H1 invalidation before M15 touch or the reverse; and
- overlapping-zone selection: return every eligible zone or deterministically select the newest
  origin with a stable key tie-break;
- H1 regime: strict last-two confirmed highs and lows, explicit unknown handling, and explicit
  mixed/equal structure handling; and
- M15 confirmation: explicit EMA initialization/crossover timing, engulfing equality,
  displacement history/median, doji semantics, candle combination, touch-bar eligibility, and
  expiry.

All diagnostic prices use arithmetic bid/ask mid. The first true range in an isolated H1 series is
high minus low; later true ranges include the prior close. These conventions are explicit research
choices and must be reconsidered when a real feed/session contract is selected.

Lifecycle replay also requires explicit first H1 and M15 cadence anchors. Zone formations are
admitted only at their recorded availability time, and a candle that began before a zone was
visible cannot affect that zone. Equal-availability observations must arrive as one immutable,
watermark-sealed receipt group so delayed H1/M15 peers cannot be applied in caller-dependent order.
The complete lifecycle policy is fingerprinted into its book, state, and emitted events.

## 7. Why StrategySpec v1 is not used yet

The current closed specification cannot faithfully encode this strategy:

- it has one bar duration rather than H1 and M15 streams;
- it has scalar indicators but no typed pivot, impulse, zone, regime, retest, or candle-pattern
  events;
- it has no persistent zone/confirmation state machine;
- its stop is only a feature multiple and its take-profit is only an initial-risk multiple;
- it cannot express structural stop/target selection, maximum daily losses, or a weekly drawdown
  guard; and
- its compiled evaluator is intentionally diagnostic-only and does not execute stops, targets,
  sizing, or account-wide risk transitions.

Creating an apparently valid v1 document would require fake rules. This intake is therefore the
honest boundary until the necessary typed capabilities exist.
